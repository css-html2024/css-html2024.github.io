How I use AI in Mangrove

Introduction

It's no secret that AI is being integrated to everywhere, especially in programming. The arrival of AI created a new era that people could build their ideas freely, even without the required knowledge. Regardless, to make things clear, I would like to declare my stance on using AI in my website in this page..

How I define 'Using AI'

What I mean by using AI, is to get assistance from generative AIs, such as asking for guidance, or to generate code or images. With this logic, tools like spellchecking are not classified into 'using AI'.

What's the deal with using AI?

Don't get me wrong, the introduction of AI has brought a significant improvement to our lives. As beginners or casual coders (like me), AI could assist us in learning the basics for coding, which lets us build the foundation of our future. However, instead of using AI as a productivity tool, most people tend to become a "meat proxy" that copies AI's answer directly to their work. That raises a question: is your work actually yours? Did you actually gain experience from it?

In addition, the rejection of using AI is also an act of boycotting their unethical training. According to phys.org, the Earth's surface is covered with 71% water and 29% dirt, and only about 3% of the water are freshwater that is suitable for humans to consume. According to the UNICEF, there are 4 billion humans experiencing severe water scarcity for at least one month each year. Despite this, large companies still decided to import large quantites of freshwater just to cool down their AI datacentre for the cheapest cost.

Furthermore, the way of them training AI also raises my concern. Since the resource used to train AI are scrapped directly from the internet, there will be a lot of creator's hard work being stolen, just to be fused into a Frankenstein which was ripped of other's work. I geneually believe that this problem should not be ignored by multi-million companies.

My reaction

To distinguish whether AI (and how much) is used in my content, most projects and web pages will come with an AI Honesty badge in either the header or the footer. The badges are classified as:

  • No AI: No Generative AI has been used in any of work
  • AI Assisted: Some part of the production has involved generative AI. It could be tinkering, advice-giving or generating a bit of code.
  • AI Generated: At least 50% of the work involved generative AI inside.

My ultimate goal is to completely ditch generative AI in my code when I get the hang of creating.
